Selling an inherited house.

We buy estate homes as-is.

When a parent or relative passes, the house often comes with a lot: belongings, repairs nobody got to, and family to agree with. You don't have to fix it or empty it. Take what you want and leave the rest.

How it works

Call us about the house. Jake comes out and walks the house with you, or with whoever is handling the estate. If we make an offer, it will explain any conditions.

About probate

We're not attorneys. If the estate is in probate, the person the court appointed usually signs. Your attorney can tell you what applies to you, and we're glad to work with them.

You decide

No charge to talk. You don't have to accept an offer.
